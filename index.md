# Longway Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 27, 2026 · **Contact:** through the Send feedback form in the app

Longway ("we") makes the Longway app for scenic drives, walking tours and location-based Stories.
This policy explains what the app collects and why. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use
analytics or tracking tools.

## Your location

- Longway uses your location to play Stories as you pass places, give turn-by-turn directions, measure
  distances to drives and tours, and record your trips. This happens **on your phone**.
- **Trip history** (routes you drove or walked) is stored **only on your phone**.
- **Convoys:** while you're in a convoy that's meeting up or on the road, your position is sent live to
  the other members of that convoy only. If you switch on meet-up sharing, your estimated arrival time is
  sent too. These live positions are **not stored** on our servers.
- **Home address (optional):** if you set one in your profile, it stays **on your phone** and is never sent
  to us.
- Location in background: used only while a Stories session, tour or drive is running.

## Your account

- To use convoys, chats and car meets you pick a **driver name**; the app creates an anonymous account.
  Optionally you can add an **email address** to keep your account on another phone.
- Your profile can include your car and the watch you wear; other Longway drivers see these in convoys
  and at car meets.

## What you share with others

- **Convoys:** name, car, chat messages and radio voice reach the members of that convoy. Radio voice is
  live only and **not recorded**.
- **Car meets:** meets you host (title, place, time) are visible to all Longway drivers; meet chat is
  visible to people who joined the meet. Chat messages are deleted after **30 days**.

## Road hazard reports

If you report a hazard while driving (for example speed bumps or a rail crossing), we receive its type,
the location where you tapped Report, the drive it was on and your account, so we can improve routes.
Reports may later be shown to other drivers as warnings, without your name.

## Feedback

If you send feedback in the app, we receive your message, the screen it came from and, only if you
choose, your location at the time.

## Microphone, motion and notifications

- **Microphone:** only while you hold Talk on a convoy's radio.
- **Motion:** to estimate steps for your tour results, on your phone.
- **Notifications:** car meet reminders, scheduled on your phone.

## Services we use

- **Supabase** stores accounts, convoys, car meets, chats and feedback, and relays live convoy positions.
- **LiveKit** carries convoy radio voice.
- **Apple Maps** provides maps, directions, places and travel times.
Each processes data only to provide those features.

## Keeping and deleting your data

You can sign out or remove your account information at any time by signing out in Profile, or by asking us through the Send feedback form in the app. When you
delete your account, your profile, convoy memberships, messages and meets are removed. Data stored only
on your phone is removed when you delete the app.

## Children

Longway isn't directed at children under 13.

## Changes

We'll update this page when the app changes what it collects and show the new date above.
